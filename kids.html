🌟 Cool Lung Facts!
Did you know these amazing things about your lungs?
🌬️
You Breathe 20,000 Times!
Every single day you take about 20,000 breaths without even thinking about it!
🎈
Lungs Are Like Balloons
Your lungs inflate and deflate just like balloons every time you breathe in and out!
🌳
Tiny Tree Branches Inside
Inside your lungs are millions of tiny branches called bronchioles — like a tree!
🏊
Size of a Swimming Pool!
If you spread out all the tiny air sacs in your lungs, they'd cover a whole tennis court!
🦁
Right Lung is Bigger
Your right lung has 3 sections (lobes) and your left lung only has 2 — to make room for your heart!
🔴
Oxygen Superhighway
Your lungs move oxygen into your blood, which carries it to every single cell in your body!
🛡️ How to Protect Your Lungs
Swipe to see things that can hurt your lungs — and how to stay safe!
🚭
Smoke
Never smoke! Smoke fills your lungs with black stuff and makes it hard to breathe.
🏭
Dirty Air
Pollution from cars and factories floats tiny bits into your lungs. Wear a mask when it's smoky!
🏠
Cookfire Smoke
Burning wood indoors creates smoke that's really bad for lungs. Open windows to let fresh air in!
🧹
Dust & Mold
Dusty or moldy rooms can irritate your lungs. Keep your room clean and let in fresh air!
🌿
Stay Active!
Running and playing makes your lungs STRONGER! Exercise is the best medicine for healthy lungs.
🥗
Eat Healthy
Fruits and veggies have vitamins that protect your lungs like a shield! Eat the rainbow!
😴
Sleep Well
Your lungs heal and recover while you sleep. Get 8-10 hours every night!
🧘 Breathing Exercise
Practice belly breathing — it's what doctors recommend to keep lungs healthy!
